# Christopher Svendsen

I make **AirwayControl**, a flight tracker for iPhone with an Apple Watch app.

Add your flight as My Flight and the app keeps gate, terminal, check-in desk and times on one card, with a Live Activity and the notifications you choose. The live map shows aircraft worldwide, drawn in the airline's own colors where we have them. When a flight is delayed or cancelled, the app shows the passenger rules for the route and fills in a claim letter you send from your own mail. It is not a claims agency and takes no fee.

No ads, no account, no tracking. The first time you open the app, you can follow one flight for free, with alerts, for 24 hours or until it lands. After that, one subscription unlocks the whole app, with a 7-day free trial for new subscribers.
